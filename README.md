# Edelweiss Mutual Fund Case Study

## About Project

**Edelweiss Mutual Fund** is one of India's leading digital-first asset management companies, offering a broad portfolio of mutual fund products including equity funds, debt funds, ETFs, SIP investments, retirement planning tools, and passive investment solutions. The platform serves retail investors, distributors, and institutional customers through web and mobile channels.

With increasing adoption of online investing and a rapidly growing investor base, the organization needed a scalable and modern digital ecosystem capable of supporting seamless mutual fund transactions, portfolio management, investor education, distributor enablement, and real-time analytics across devices.

The vision was to create a secure, cloud-enabled, and customer-centric investment platform that simplified digital investing while maintaining compliance, operational efficiency, and enterprise-grade security standards.

## Problem

**Could not handle peak demand**  
The legacy infrastructure struggled to support growing investor traffic during high-volume investment windows such as New Fund Offerings (NFOs), resulting in slow response times and inconsistent platform performance.

**Poor investor experience on web and mobile**  
Existing systems delivered fragmented user experiences with slow workflows, confusing navigation, and unreliable transaction handling across desktop and mobile platforms, impacting customer satisfaction and retention.

**Fragmented technology ecosystem**  
Multiple disconnected systems for transactions, reporting, portfolio tracking, customer support, and distributor operations created operational inefficiencies and data silos.

**Security and compliance risks**  
Legacy systems lacked modern cybersecurity standards and secure architecture practices needed to protect sensitive financial and investor information.

**Operational management challenges**  
Internal teams faced difficulties managing deployments, scaling infrastructure, monitoring services, and maintaining consistent business continuity across systems.

**High maintenance costs**  
Monolithic architecture and outdated technologies increased maintenance efforts, release timelines, infrastructure expenses, and dependency on specialized legacy resources.

**Data stuck in silos**  
Critical investment and customer data remained isolated across multiple systems, limiting actionable insights, reporting accuracy, and business intelligence capabilities.

**Reduced customer satisfaction**  
Slow systems, inconsistent digital experiences, and lack of intuitive workflows negatively impacted investor trust and overall platform engagement.

## The Client

**Edelweiss Mutual Fund** is part of the broader Edelweiss Financial Services group and provides a wide range of investment products and wealth-building solutions to retail and institutional investors across India. The company focuses heavily on digital investment experiences, SIP-based investing, investor education, and scalable financial infrastructure.

## The Solution

A complete digital transformation initiative was implemented to modernize the mutual fund investment ecosystem and deliver a scalable, secure, and high-performance investment experience across web and mobile platforms.

- **Unified Investment Platform:** Centralized ecosystem enabling investors to manage SIPs, purchases, redemptions, portfolio tracking, calculators, and financial planning tools from a single platform.
- **SIP and Investment Automation:** Advanced SIP workflows, automated investment journeys, portfolio monitoring, and transaction tracking for seamless investor engagement.
- **Intelligent Reporting Dashboards:** Business intelligence dashboards with real-time analytics, interactive portfolio snapshots, and investment insights for smarter financial decisions.
- **Enhanced Portfolio Management:** Intuitive experiences allowing users to monitor performance, compare market movements, and access fund insights in real time.
- **Investor Education Ecosystem:** Calculators, webinars, podcasts, educational content, and market insights to improve investor awareness and engagement.
- **Self-Service Utilities:** Customers can independently update nominees, bank accounts, personal information, and investment preferences without manual support dependency.
- **Mobile-First Investment Experience:** Responsive mobile applications with secure authentication, transaction management, portfolio tracking, and instant access to investment services.
- **Cloud-Native Infrastructure:** Migrated to AWS-based cloud architecture for scalability, high availability, disaster recovery, and continuous performance optimization.
- **Data-Driven Operations:** Centralized reporting systems and analytics pipelines improved operational visibility, business intelligence, customer analytics, and strategic decision-making.
- **Enterprise-Grade Security:** HTTPS encryption, DevSecOps workflows, secure authentication systems, and compliance-ready architecture to strengthen data security and platform reliability.

## Result

- **Faster time-to-market for new fund offerings** — Improved platform scalability and deployment processes enabled quicker launches for new investment products and NFO campaigns.
- **Faster mutual fund transaction processing** — Streamlined transaction systems significantly reduced delays in purchases, redemptions, SIP processing, and investor onboarding workflows.
- **Improved investor engagement** — Enhanced digital experiences, self-service tools, educational content, and intelligent dashboards increased customer engagement across web and mobile channels.
- **Operational efficiency through automation** — Automation of internal workflows, centralized infrastructure management, and CI/CD pipelines reduced operational overhead and manual effort.
- **Stronger infrastructure management** — Cloud-native architecture improved uptime, scalability, platform resilience, and performance during peak investor traffic periods.
- **Compliance and security improvements** — Implementation of modern security standards and DevSecOps practices improved compliance readiness and strengthened investor trust.
- **Scalable digital ecosystem** — The new digital ecosystem was designed to support long-term growth, high transaction volumes, and evolving business requirements while maintaining consistent user experiences.
- **Security-by-design architecture** — A modern, secure-by-design platform approach minimized cybersecurity risks and improved protection of sensitive financial information.
